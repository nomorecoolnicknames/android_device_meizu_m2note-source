m2note LineageOS 20 (Treble) — variant "fit1536"
================================================

This build is the TRIMMED variant of lineage_m2note: its /system is shaped to
fit the 1536 MiB stock system partition (p23) of the Meizu M2 Note (M571)
without repartitioning the eMMC.

Removed, via LOCAL_OVERRIDES_MODULES in device/meizu/m2note/Android.mk
(module m2note_fit1536_trim):

  NfcNci                     ~13 MiB   no NFC controller on this unit
  ThemePicker                ~30 MiB   Styles & wallpapers picker
  Seedvault                  ~20 MiB   encrypted backup app
  StorageManager             ~12 MiB   Settings "free up space"
  EmergencyInfo              ~12 MiB   emergency info editor
  Gallery2                   ~12 MiB   stock gallery
  NotoSerifCJK-Regular.ttc   ~24 MiB   CJK serif font
  NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc    ~19 MiB   CJK sans font

Consequence you can see from the UI: Chinese / Japanese / Korean text renders
as tofu boxes, there is no wallpaper or style picker, no gallery app, no
backup app.  Other hardware and software behavior still requires validation.
